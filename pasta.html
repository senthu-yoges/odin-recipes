Cajun Chicken Pasta

Home

Description

A creamy Cajun chicken pasta filled with bell peppers and mushrooms. This recipe is packed with spicy flavor!

Ingredients

  • 4 ouncees linguine or other pasta
  • 2 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
  • 2 teaspoons Cajun seasoning
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 red bell pepper,sliced
  • 1 green bell pepper,sliced
  • 4 fresh mushrooms,sliced
  • 1 green onion,chopped
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • One quarter teaspoon dried basil
  • One quarter teaspoon lemon pepper
  • One quarter teaspoon salt
  • One eigth teaspoon garlic powder
  • One eigth teaspoon ground black pepper
  • One quarter cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients
  2. Boil a large pot of lightly salted water. Add your pasta and cook until al dente. This should take 8 to 10 minutes. Drain water.
  3. Cut your chicken breast into strips and place them into a plastic zipper bag. Add Cajun seasoning and shake to coat the chicken.
  4. In a large skilled melt butter over medium heat. Add your chicken and cook until browned while stirring. After 5 to 7 minutes ensure the chicken is cooked throughly.
  5. Add bell peppers, mushrooms and green onion. Cook and stir them for 2 to 3 minutes.
  6. Reduce the heat and stir in cream,basil,lemon pepper,salt,garlic powder,and black pepper. Add cooked pasta, while stirring everything together.
  7. Springle Parmesan cheese
  8. Serve and enjoy!
